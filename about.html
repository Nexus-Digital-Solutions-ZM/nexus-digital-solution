Nexus Digital Solutions is a Zambian technology company incorporated in 2026 and registered under the Patents and Companies Registration Agency (PACRA).

The name "Nexus" reflects the company's core philosophy: to serve as the connection point between underserved communities and transformative digital tools. In a landscape where millions of Zambians remain excluded from formal financial systems and lack access to data-driven decision-making tools, Nexus Digital Solutions exists to bridge that gap.

We don't build technology for technology's sake. We build tools that solve real problems, reduce friction in people's daily economic lives, and create tangible value for the communities we serve. Every product under the Nexus banner is designed with practical deployment in mind — targeting real users across formal and informal sectors of the Zambian economy.

Primary operations are based in Lusaka, Zambia, with products designed for scalability across Sub-Saharan Africa. International client engagements are conducted remotely.

Core Values

What Drives Everything We Build

⚡

Innovation

We embrace emerging technologies not as trends, but as instruments of meaningful change. We build what does not yet exist when existing solutions fall short.

Inclusion

Our products are designed for the many, not the few. We prioritize accessibility, low-cost deployment, and usability for people across all economic levels.

Simeon Mwale is a final-year Computer Science student at DMI St. Eugene University in Lusaka, Zambia — and the founder and lead engineer of Nexus Digital Solutions.

Simeon brings hands-on experience in full-stack software development and field data work with the Zambia Statistics Agency during the national census. He is an active participant in the Bitcoin Zambia and BitDevs Zambia communities, where he engages in mentorship and grassroots technology education.

His approach to entrepreneurship is grounded in building for real users. Every product under Nexus Digital Solutions has been shaped by direct observation of market gaps in Zambia's informal economy and agricultural sector — not assumptions made from a desk.

Simeon also freelances on international platforms including Upwork and Fiverr, bringing global client experience back into the products and services Nexus delivers locally.
