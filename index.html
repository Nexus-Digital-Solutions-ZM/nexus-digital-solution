About Us

Built on the
Principle of Connection

Nexus Digital Solutions is a Zambian technology company incorporated in 2026 and registered under PACRA. We exist to serve as the connection point between underserved communities and transformative digital tools.

Where millions remain excluded from formal systems and lack access to data-driven tools, Nexus bridges that gap — from cloud infrastructure to AI-powered platforms, each product is built with real users in mind.
